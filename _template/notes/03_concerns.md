# Concerns

One entry per concern. Each becomes a row in `registry/issues.csv`.

Format:

### <short title>
- Code: <taxonomy code>  — Severity: critical | major | minor — Evidence: confirmed | inferred | needs-data
- Location: Table/Figure/Section
- What: one or two sentences, numbers included.
- Why it matters: what conclusion it undermines.
- How to check further: recomputation, data request, re-analysis.
